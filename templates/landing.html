Flexible simulation inputs

Set initial price, expected return, volatility, time horizon, and number of simulations (up to 10,000 paths). Choose Normal, Student-t (fat-tailed), or bootstrap sampling from your own uploaded historical data.

Charts & statistical summaries

See sample simulated price paths and the distribution of outcomes, alongside mean, median, standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis, Value-at-Risk, Conditional VaR, and percentile breakdowns -- with outlier paths automatically flagged.

Save, reopen & export

Save simulations to revisit later, delete what you don't need, and export results and summaries as CSV or JSON, or charts as PNG -- all encrypted at rest.

What this tool is (and isn't)

Monte Carlo Studio is an analytical and educational tool for exploring how randomness, drift, and volatility shape a distribution of possible outcomes. It validates that your inputs are well-formed, but it cannot judge whether the numbers you enter are realistic for any real-world asset -- that judgment is yours. It reports statistics about the simulation you configured; it does not predict real markets or provide investment, financial, or trading advice.
