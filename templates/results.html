Skewness ?Measures asymmetry in the outcomes. Positive means a longer tail toward higher prices; negative means a longer tail toward lower prices; near zero means roughly symmetric.
{{ "%.3f"|format(summary.skewness) }}
Excess kurtosis ?Measures how heavy the tails of the distribution are compared to a normal distribution. Higher values mean extreme outcomes were more common in this simulation than a normal bell curve would predict.
{{ "%.3f"|format(summary.excess_kurtosis) }}
Value at Risk (95%) ?The simulated loss (as a % of initial price) that is exceeded in only 5% of simulated outcomes. A statistical description of this simulation's spread, not a prediction or recommendation.

Value at Risk (99%) ?The simulated loss (as a % of initial price) that is exceeded in only 1% of simulated outcomes -- a stricter, more extreme threshold than the 95% figure.
